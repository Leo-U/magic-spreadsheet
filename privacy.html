Privacy and your data

Updated 26 October 2026

Guest mode

Your question sets, answers, marks and progress are stored in this browser. Guest study data is not uploaded to Google when you sign in. You can export a backup or deliberately import it into your signed-in collection.

Google sign-in and sync

If you choose Google sign-in, the app uses your Google account identifier and verified email to open the correct study collection. It requests permission to create and manage the Google Drive files you use with this app (drive.file), together with basic account identification. It does not request access to all your Drive files or all your spreadsheets.

Your signed-in study data is saved in a Google Sheet in your account. Changes also stay in this browser for offline use and recovery. The Sheet keeps earlier revisions, so deleting a question in the app hides it but does not erase it from older saved versions.

Storage and access

This is a static website: study records and Google access tokens are not sent to an application server operated by Magic Spreadsheet. Google handles sign-in and stores the synced spreadsheet. GitHub Pages hosts the website and receives ordinary web requests under its own privacy policy.

A temporary Google access token is retained in this tab's session storage so refreshing can reopen your collection. It expires and is cleared by signing out. The app remembers the last study collection on this device so you can keep studying and marking questions when Google is disconnected. This does not grant Google access: reconnecting verifies the same account before syncing. Sign-out stops automatic reopening, but account study caches and recovery copies can remain in local browser storage. Use a trusted device, and clear this site's browsing data to remove those local copies after exporting any unsynced work you want to keep.

Magic Spreadsheet does not sell study data or use it for advertising. The app includes no analytics trackers. Google services and external links are governed by those providers' policies.

Disconnecting and deleting

Use Sign out to disconnect this tab. You can also revoke Magic Spreadsheet's access through your Google account connections. To remove your cloud study data, delete its spreadsheet in Google Drive and empty its trash when you are ready. Removing browser data and deleting cloud data are separate actions. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Questions

Contact the maintainer through the project issue tracker. Do not post private study data or account credentials there.
